What does Mmakosha Tombstones specialize in?

We specialize in designing, installing, restoring and funeral tombstones. We provide high quality granite tombstones made with care and professional workmanship.

When was the business established?

Mmakosha Tombstones was established in 2018 and has been serving families with trusted memorial solutions since then.

Do you use granite tombstones?

Yes, we provide high quality granite tombstones that are durable, elegant and suitable for long-lasting memorials.

Where are you located?

We are located at 600 Ga Mothapo, Tholongwe Street, 0734.

How can I contact you?

You can call us on 071 312 1330 for service inquiries, quotations and support.

Do you offer tombstone restoration?

Yes, we restore tombstones to improve their appearance and preserve their memorial significance.

Do you install tombstones?

Yes, we offer professional tombstone installation services carried out with care, precision and respect.

How do I get a quote?

You can contact us directly by phone to discuss your needs and receive a quote based on the design, material and installation requirements.

When will my order be processed?

Order timelines depend on the design, material and installation requirements. We will guide you on the process once your request has been confirmed.

Can I ask about custom memorial options?

Yes, we welcome custom requests and will work with you to create a suitable memorial design that matches your needs.
